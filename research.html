Research framework

From question
to evidence.

Our research framework begins with the conditions affecting a market and builds toward a reasoned interpretation. Every step should distinguish observation from inference, and signal from noise.

01 / CONTEXT

Establish the environment

Analyze liquidity, volatility, market structure, macro conditions and relevant developments before isolating a potential signal.

02 / VALIDATION

Test competing cases

Evaluate whether the evidence supports an interpretation, and identify the conditions under which it would be weakened.

03 / REVIEW

Adapt to new information

Research remains subject to revision. New data, changing participation and unexpected events can alter a previously reasonable view.

Risk awareness

Clarity includes
what may go wrong.

Financial markets are uncertain. A responsible research process considers market volatility, liquidity gaps, correlation shifts and scenarios that do not match the central thesis.

The goal is not to eliminate uncertainty but to understand where it matters, communicate limitations and avoid presenting probabilistic judgments as certainties.
